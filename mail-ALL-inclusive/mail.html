A New Chapter in Los Cabos

All-Inclusive, The Hacienda Way

All-Inclusive should mean more than convenience.
It should mean freedom — the freedom to savor slowly,
to explore deeply, to linger a little longer.

At Hacienda del Mar, this new chapter unfolds along the shores of the Sea of Cortez, where the timeless architecture of a Mexican hacienda meets the ease of a fully inclusive escape. Warm courtyards, colorful tiles, and oceanfront views remain at the heart of the experience — now accompanied by a seamless way to enjoy it all.

Culinary Without Limits

From Baja seafood inspired by the Sea of Cortez to the vibrant flavors of regional Mexican cuisine, every meal becomes part of the story. Handcrafted cocktails, curated dining venues, and spontaneous indulgences are no longer choices to calculate — they are moments to embrace.

A Sense of Place

In a destination known for sleek, contemporary beachfront resorts, Hacienda del Mar offers something rare — a setting inspired by the timeless character of a traditional Mexican hacienda.

Here, architecture tells one story.
The All-Inclusive experience completes it.
